{{tag:FAQs|help}}

What owners and developers ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Can you renovate a building that stays occupied?

Yes. We build a phasing plan around occupancy, coordinate notices with your management team and keep common areas clean and safe throughout.

Can you design finish packages at different price points?

Yes. We typically develop two or three packages so you can compare cost against expected rent before committing, then build a model unit to confirm the look.

Will you work with our architect or general contractor?

Yes. On several San Diego communities we delivered site and landscape design alongside the project's building designer and general contractor. We can lead the whole renovation or take a defined scope.

Can you repair items from our SB 721 balcony inspection?

Yes. We repair and rebuild decks, stairs, walkways and balconies, including items identified in an exterior elevated element inspection report. Send us the report and we'll price the repairs.

What size properties do you work on?

The communities we've designed range from 7 to 36 units. We're a good fit for owners who want senior attention and design input rather than a national production crew.
