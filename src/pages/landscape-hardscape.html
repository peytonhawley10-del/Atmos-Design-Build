{{tag:FAQs|help}}

Landscape and hardscape questions

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Do you design and build, or just design?{{icon:plus}}

Both. We provide site and landscape design on its own, and we build landscape and hardscape as part of a renovation that includes other trades. Where a project is landscape only, we bring in a licensed landscape contractor to install our design.

Can you design a low-maintenance, low-water landscape?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Drought-tolerant and native planting with efficient irrigation is our default for Southern California properties.

Do you work with existing outdoor spaces?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Most of our work is renewing what's already there: replacing tired planting, repairing hardscape, adding lighting and creating usable amenity space.

Do you offer ongoing landscape maintenance?{{icon:plus}}

We don't provide routine maintenance. We hand over a maintenance guide and recommendations so your landscape service can keep it thriving.

Can you add amenities like a fire pit, outdoor kitchen or bocce court?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Outdoor kitchens, fire features, pergolas, bocce courts, turf lawns and lounge areas are all things we design into multifamily and hospitality properties.
