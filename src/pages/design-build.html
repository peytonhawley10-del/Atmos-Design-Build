Design questions

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Can I hire Atmos for design only, or build only?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We can prepare the design for another contractor to build, or build from plans you already have. Most clients find they get the best result, and the fewest gaps, when we do both.

Will you work with our designer, architect or brand standards?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We regularly work alongside building designers and general contractors, and we'll build to a brand's design standards or PIP.

Can we see a 3D rendering before we commit?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We provide 3D models and renderings so you can see the space before construction begins.

Can we make changes during design?{{icon:plus}}

Of course. That's what the design phase is for. We revise until you're confident, then lock the specification before ordering.

Do you handle purchasing of materials and furniture?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We can manage procurement of the materials, finishes and furniture your project needs through trusted suppliers.
