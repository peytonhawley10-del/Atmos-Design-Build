{{tag:FAQs|help}}

What hotel owners and GMs ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Have you renovated hotels before?

Our completed work is apartment-community site and amenity design, interior renovations and outdoor living design, and every project page states exactly what we did. Hotels are a new focus for us, which is why we start with a model room: you approve one finished room, its schedule and its cost before committing to the rest.

Can you renovate while the hotel stays open?

Yes. We plan hotel work in phases by floor, wing or room block, with work hours, access routes and noise limits agreed with your GM before we start, and a weekly count of rooms out of service.

Can you build to our brand's PIP?

Yes. Send us the property improvement plan and we'll price it line by line, flag long-lead items early and schedule against your brand's deadline. Brand approvals and inspections stay with you and your brand; we build to what they approve.

Will you build a model room first?

Yes, and we recommend it. A model room lets ownership, the brand and operations approve the finished result before we repeat it across the property.

Do you handle furniture, fixtures and equipment?

We coordinate delivery, receiving and installation of FF&E and soft goods with your purchasing agent or designer, so each room is complete at turnover. For independent hotels we can also coordinate selection and ordering with your designer or purchasing agent, priced line by line.

Do you handle accessibility upgrades?

Yes. Accessible guestroom and path-of-travel improvements are often triggered by renovation work, and we include them in the scope. Where the permit needs stamped drawings, they are prepared by a licensed design professional, yours or one we bring in, and we price and build to them.

We're a small independent hotel. Is that a fit?

Yes. Independent and boutique properties get a distinctive look and a single team responsible for delivering it.
