{{tag:FAQs|help}}

What hotel owners and GMs ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Can you renovate while the hotel stays open?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. That's how we plan every hospitality project: phased by floor, wing or room block, with work hours, access routes and noise limits agreed with your GM before we start.

Can you build to our brand's PIP?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Send us the property improvement plan and we'll price it line by line, flag long-lead items early and schedule against your brand's deadline.

Will you build a model room first?{{icon:plus}}

Yes, and we recommend it. A model room lets ownership, the brand and operations approve the finished result before we repeat it across the property.

Do you handle furniture, fixtures and equipment?{{icon:plus}}

We coordinate delivery and installation with your purchasing agent or designer so each room is complete at turnover. For independent hotels we can also manage selection and procurement of furniture and finishes.

Do you handle accessibility upgrades?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Accessible guestroom and path-of-travel improvements are often triggered by renovation work, and we include them in the scope and permit documents.

We're a small independent hotel. Is that a fit?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Independent and boutique properties are where our design-and-build approach adds the most: you get a distinctive look and a single team responsible for delivering it.
