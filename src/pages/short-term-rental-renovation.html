{{tag:FAQs|help}}

What rental owners and managers ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
How long will my rental be offline?{{icon:plus}}

It depends on scope. We give you a day-by-day schedule with the bid so you can block the right dates, and we order materials ahead so the work starts the day the calendar opens.

Can you manage the project if I'm not local?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Many owners manage from a distance. You'll have one contact, regular photo updates and a final walk-through by video if you can't be there.

Do you help with design and furnishing?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We provide design concepts, material selection and renderings. We can also order the furniture and finishes in the approved design and have them installed, so the property is guest-ready at handover. Linens, kitchenware and listing photography stay with you or your manager.

Can you add value outside as well as inside?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Outdoor living is one of our strengths: decks, patios, outdoor kitchens, fire features and low-maintenance landscape that make a listing stand out.

Which areas do you cover?{{icon:plus}}

All of San Diego County, including the beach communities and North County.

Do you work with property management companies that run many rentals?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. For managers with a portfolio, we set a finish standard and per-scope pricing so upgrades are consistent and predictable from one property to the next.
