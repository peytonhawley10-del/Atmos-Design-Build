Set up to be your preferred vendor

The paperwork and communication around the work are what get a vendor called back.

  1. Vendor paperwork without the back-and-forth

    Certificate of insurance, W-9 and license on request. Send us your additional-insured and endorsement requirements and we'll confirm them with our broker before you add us to a bid list. We'll register in the vendor compliance platform you use.

  2. Pricing you can budget with

    Per-unit and per-scope pricing for repeat work, so next year's capital plan isn't a guess.

  3. A finish standard for the property

    One specification for flooring, paint, fixtures and hardware, so every turn matches and reorders are simple.

  4. Small jobs covered too

    One-off repairs and work orders go to {{handyman}}. Send them through the same form and we'll route them.

{{tag:Occupied-unit protocol|home}}

How we work when residents are home

The same steps on every occupied unit, agreed with your office before we start, so there is nothing for you to explain afterward.

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid|blue}}

  1. Notice schedule set with your office

    We agree entry dates in advance so your team can serve resident notices on time, and we tell you who will be on site each day. We don't enter a unit that hasn't been noticed.

  2. Photo log before we touch anything

    Existing conditions are photographed at the start of work in every unit.

  3. Protection and dust control

    Floors, furnishings and common paths are covered, and dusty work is contained.

  4. Keys and lockup

    Keys are signed out and back each day, and every unit is locked when we leave.

  5. Clean and reported daily

    Work areas are cleaned at the end of each day and your office gets a status update.

{{tag:FAQs|help}}

What property managers ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Can you work in occupied units?

Yes. We follow the same five steps on every occupied unit: agreed notice schedule, photo log, protection and dust control, key control, and daily cleanup with a status update. See the occupied-unit protocol above.

Will you register in our vendor compliance system?

Yes. Tell us which platform your company uses and we'll complete registration and upload insurance and tax documents so we're approved before work starts.

Can you give us per-unit pricing?

Yes. For repeatable scopes such as flooring, paint, kitchens and baths, we price by unit type so you can plan turns and upgrades across the property.

How quickly can you turn a unit?

You get a day-by-day schedule with each bid, so you know the make-ready date before you approve the work. A paint-and-flooring turn moves much faster than a full kitchen and bath upgrade, and we order materials ahead so the unit isn't waiting on deliveries.

Do you take small repair requests?

Small repairs and single work orders are handled by {{handyman}}. Send them through the same form and we'll route them.

Do you work with HOAs and commercial properties?

Yes. We renovate common areas, exteriors and grounds for HOAs and mixed-use properties as well as apartment communities. We also have pages for multifamily owners, hotels and short-term rentals.
