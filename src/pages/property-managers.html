What property managers ask us

{{btn:bid|Request a Bid}}
Can you work in occupied units?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We schedule around residents, coordinate entry notices with your office, protect floors and furnishings, keep to agreed hours and clean up daily.

Will you register in our vendor compliance system?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Tell us which platform your company uses and we'll complete registration and upload insurance and tax documents so we're approved before work starts.

Can you give us per-unit pricing?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. For repeatable scopes such as flooring, paint, kitchens and baths, we price by unit type so you can plan turns and upgrades across the property.

How quickly can you turn a unit?{{icon:plus}}

It depends on the scope and material lead times. A paint-and-flooring refresh moves much faster than a full kitchen and bath upgrade. We give you a day-by-day schedule with the bid and pre-order materials so the unit isn't waiting on deliveries.

Do you take small repair requests?{{icon:plus}}

Small repairs and single work orders are handled by {{handyman}}. Send them through the same form and we'll route them.

Do you work with HOAs and commercial properties?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We renovate common areas, exteriors and grounds for HOAs and mixed-use properties as well as apartment communities.
