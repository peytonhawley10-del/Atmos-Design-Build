What you get

Decisions made on paper, where they're cheap to change

Changing a tile on a finish board costs nothing. Changing it after it's installed in forty units costs a great deal. Our design process is built to get the decisions right first.

Design concepts & space planning

Layouts and concepts for units, rooms, common areas and outdoor spaces, tailored to who you want to attract.

Finish & material boards

Flooring, tile, counters, cabinets, fixtures, lighting and paint, specified by product so they can be priced and reordered.

3D renderings

Realistic views of the finished space to share with ownership, investors, brands and lenders.

Budget alignment

Design and estimating happen together, so the concept you fall for is one you can afford to build.

Construction documents & permits

Drawings and submittals for the scope, coordinated with engineers or your architect where the project calls for one.

Turnkey build-out

The same team manages construction, so nothing is lost in a handoff between designer and contractor.

Why it matters to you

Design that's practical for managed properties

  1. Approvals get easier

    Owners, asset managers and brands sign off faster when they can see the result.

    A written finish standard means the tenth unit looks like the first, and the next turn is a reorder.

  3. Lead times don't surprise you

    We select readily available products and order before demolition.

  4. Finishes hold up

    Materials are chosen for durability and cleaning, not only for looks.

FAQs

Design questions

Request a Bid
Can I hire Atmos for design only, or build only?

Yes. We can prepare the design for another contractor to build, or build from plans you already have. Most clients find they get the best result, and the fewest gaps, when we do both.

Will you work with our designer, architect or brand standards?

Yes. We regularly work alongside building designers and general contractors, and we'll build to a brand's design standards or PIP.

Can we see a 3D rendering before we commit?

Yes. We provide 3D models and renderings so you can see the space before construction begins.

Can we make changes during design?

Of course. That's what the design phase is for. We revise until you're confident, then lock the specification before ordering.

Do you handle purchasing of materials and furniture?

Yes. We can manage procurement of the materials, finishes and furniture your project needs through trusted suppliers.
