FAQs

Interior renovation questions

Request a Bid
Can you renovate units while neighbors are home?

Yes. We schedule noisy work within agreed hours, protect corridors and elevators, and clean up daily so residents and guests are disturbed as little as possible.

Can you match the finishes in units we've already renovated?

Yes. We'll document your existing standard, source matching or equivalent materials, and build a finish schedule so future units stay consistent.

Do interior renovations need permits?

Cosmetic work usually doesn't. Changes to plumbing, electrical, mechanical systems or walls often do. We tell you up front what your scope requires and handle the submittal.

Do you supply materials or can we?

Either. We can procure everything, or install owner-supplied materials if you have national accounts or brand-specified items.
