Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

At Atmos Design + Build, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

Personal information

  • Name, company, role, phone number and email address.
  • Details about your property and project, such as property type, location, scope, timeline and budget.
  • Payment and billing information, if you become a client.

Non-personal information

  • Browser type and operating system.
  • IP address and general location.
  • Usage data, including how you interact with our website.

2. How we use your information

  • Responding to you: to reply to bid requests and inquiries and to schedule property walks.
  • Service delivery: to provide design, renovation and related services.
  • Billing and payments: to process payments and send invoices.
  • Marketing: to share updates about our services. You can opt out at any time.
  • Website improvement: to understand how the site is used and improve it.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers: vendors who help us operate, such as our website host and form processor, email provider, payment processors and IT support.
  • Our sister handyman company: when you ask about a small repair or work order that it is better placed to handle.
  • Project collaborators: contractors or consultants working on your project, with your consent.
  • Legal compliance: authorities or legal entities if required by law or to protect our rights and property.

4. Data storage and security

  • Retention: we keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
  • Security: we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and restricted access.

5. Your rights

  • Access: request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correction: update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your information, subject to legal obligations.
  • Objection: opt out of specific uses of your data, such as marketing communications.

To exercise your rights, contact us at info@atmosbuild.com.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the site to function and, if analytics are enabled, to measure usage and improve performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Our website does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

7. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact us
