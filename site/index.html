FAQs

Questions managers and owners ask us

Don't see yours? Call (619) 736-7590

Do you work in occupied buildings and hotels that stay open?

Yes. We plan renovations around occupancy. Work is phased by unit, floor or building, entry notices are coordinated with your team (including the written notice California requires before entering a rental unit), work hours are agreed up front, and the site is cleaned daily.

Can you handle both the design and the construction?

Yes. Design concepts, material selection, drawings, permits and construction all run through one team. If you already have plans or a brand standard, we'll build to them. If you only need design, we do that too.

Do you provide a COI and W-9, and will you register in our vendor system?

Yes. We send a certificate of insurance, W-9 and license information on request, can list your management company and ownership entity on the certificate as your contract requires, and will register in the vendor compliance platform you use.

What size projects do you take on?

From a single unit or guestroom refresh to multi-building interior, exterior and landscape programs. For one-off repairs and small work orders, we'll route you to our sister handyman company.

How do you price renovations?

After a property walk you get an itemized written bid. For repeatable scopes like unit upgrades, we price per unit or per room type so you can budget across the property. Any change is priced and approved in writing before work proceeds.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We prepare and submit what the scope requires and coordinate inspections through sign-off.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Atmos Design + Build holds California General Building Contractor license #1154511 and carries liability insurance. You can verify the license with the CSLB at any time.

Can you keep finishes consistent across units or match a brand standard?

Yes. We build a finish schedule for the property so every unit or room is renovated to the same specification, and reorders are simple. For branded hotels we price and build against your PIP or design standards.

Which areas do you serve?

San Diego County is home base, and we take on projects across Southern California.
