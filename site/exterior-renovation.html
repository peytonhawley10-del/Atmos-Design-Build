California SB 721

Have a balcony inspection report with repairs on it?

California requires periodic inspection of decks, balconies, stairs and walkways on apartment buildings with three or more units. If your report came back with repair items, we can price and complete them.

  • Send us the inspection report for an itemized repair bid
  • Framing, waterproofing, finishes and railings under one contract
  • Permits and inspections coordinated through sign-off
  • Resident access planned unit by unit

FAQs

Exterior renovation questions

Request a Bid
Can you combine exterior work with landscape and interiors?

Yes, and it's usually more efficient. One mobilization, one schedule and one point of contact for the building, the grounds and the units.

Do you perform SB 721 inspections?

No. Inspections are performed by a qualified inspector you engage. We take the resulting report and carry out the repairs.

How do you keep residents safe during exterior work?

Work areas are fenced or taped off, alternate routes are signed, and stairs and balconies are only taken out of service with notice and a plan for access.

Will you help choose colors and materials?

Yes. We develop color and material schemes and can provide renderings so ownership can approve the look before work begins.
