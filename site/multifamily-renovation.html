Value-add, end to end

Four scopes that move rents, managed as one

Owners often hire a different firm for each of these. We design them as one program and build them on one schedule, so nothing is missed where the scopes meet.

Unit interiors

Classic-to-renovated upgrades with defined finish packages: kitchens, baths, flooring, lighting and paint, priced per unit type.

Amenities & common areas

Leasing offices, clubrooms, fitness, corridors and outdoor amenity spaces that give prospects a reason to choose you.

Exteriors & curb appeal

Paint, siding, entries, lighting, fencing, and the deck, stair and balcony repairs that protect the building.

Landscape & hardscape

Courtyards, pool decks, private yards and low-water planting. Site and landscape design is where Atmos has its deepest multifamily portfolio.

Design-led

A finish standard you can underwrite

We don't start with demolition. We start with a plan and a finish board, so you know what each unit will look like and cost before you commit capital.

  • A model unit built first, then repeated to the same spec
  • Renderings for investor and lender presentations

Occupied renovations

Many residents stay through a renovation. We plan the work so they barely have to think about it.

    Which units and buildings, in what order, and how long each is offline.

    Common areas are cleared and cleaned at the end of each shift.

FAQs

What owners and developers ask us

Request a Bid
Can you renovate a building that stays occupied?

Yes. We build a phasing plan around occupancy, coordinate notices with your management team and keep common areas clean and safe throughout.

Can you design finish packages at different price points?

Yes. We typically develop two or three packages so you can compare cost against expected rent before committing, then build a model unit to confirm the look.

Will you work with our architect or general contractor?

Yes. On several San Diego communities we delivered site and landscape design alongside the project's building designer and general contractor. We can lead the whole renovation or take a defined scope.

Can you repair items from our SB 721 balcony inspection?

Yes. We repair and rebuild decks, stairs, walkways and balconies, including items identified in an exterior elevated element inspection report. Send us the report and we'll price the repairs.

What size properties do you work on?

The communities we've designed range from 7 to 36 units. We're a good fit for owners who want senior attention and design input rather than a national production crew.
